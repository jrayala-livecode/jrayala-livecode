# Joaquín Ayala
### Entrepreneur · Software developer · Culture builder
I build technology that strengthens communities, especially Chile's independent music and culture scene. I'm the founder of [Zonora](https://zonora.cl), a social agenda where artists, venues, collectives and audiences meet, built to give independent culture the visibility and tools it usually lacks.
My work sits where **strategy, product and code** meet. I find what's holding a project or a scene back, design the strategy to grow it and then build it myself, from the backend to the last UX detail. I also lead small teams building internal tools that automate bottlenecks and help people focus on what matters.
**What I do**
- **Entrepreneurship and strategy:** product vision, business models, go-to-market, growth
- **Culture and community:** empowering independent artists, venues and cultural spaces
- **Product and engineering:** end-to-end builds with Laravel, FastAPI, Python, Vue/Nuxt and Electron
- **Automation and AI:** data pipelines, scrapers, and AI agents integrated where they add real value
**How I work**
Fast iterations, clear decisions and things that actually get used. Technology is the means; impact is the goal.
